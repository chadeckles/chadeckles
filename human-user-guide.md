# Human User Guide: @chadeckles

### What are some honest, unfiltered things about you?

- I generally consider myself an optimist. In a world that sometimes treats a sunny disposition as a form of weakness, I find this challenging at times but always try to embrace the positive things in life.

### What drives you nuts?

- Showing up to a meeting that has no agenda.
- Lack of ownership.
- Scheduling meetings in the late afternoon or evening on a Friday 🙄
- Apathy for important security concerns that should be a priority.

### What are your quirks?

- I often will try to inject some humor into the mix to lighten the mood - those jokes don't always land 😬
- While I enjoy communicating and socializing with others, I tend to get my energy from quiet time where I can reflect solo. #introvert-lite
- I am a huge fan of to-do lists and find myself being productive and happy when I can leverage them effectively. 

### What are some things that people might misunderstand about you that you should clarify?

- During meetings and interactions, I tend to ask a lot of questions...sometimes in a Socratic way. This is not always because I don't understand something, but because I may feel a topic that needs clarity and/or further discussion.

## About my colleagues

### What qualities do you particularly value in your colleagues?

- Asking open-ended questions and embracing collaboration.
- Showing care and respect for one another.
- Supporting other colleagues in times of need.

### What do you wish colleagues didn’t do?

- Commandeering a group call so others don't have an opportunity to be heard.
- Unrelated multitasking when we are having a direct conversation.
- Not recognizing others for their hard work and effort.

### How can people earn an extra gold star with you?

- Be action-oriented and display ownership.
- Take the extra few minutes to provide clear and purposeful instructions versus fuzzy, generic comments.
- Embody a growth mindset without the ego.


## About my interactions

### What’s the best way to communicate with you?

- Slack is typically the best way to communicate with me for prompt and frequent messages.
- If the message is more detailed and necessitates future reference, email is the best.
- For more complex matters that require collaboration and group input, Zoom meetings are preferred.

### What’s the best way to convince you to do something?

- Anchor it with vision and impact ⚓️

### How do you like to give feedback?

- Directly and in-the-moment, as much as possible
- 1:1 Zoom meetings for more sensitive conversations
- With tones of empathy and respect

### How do you like to get feedback?

The same as I give feedback (see above).
